What this does and does not do

Rules remove names, record numbers, dates, contact details, addresses, facilities and ages over 89, and flag leftover proper nouns for you. Rules miss things: a misspelled name, a nickname, a rare diagnosis paired with a small town. Read the scrubbed text before you copy it — you are the final check. This is a workflow safeguard, not a certified de-identification method; your institution's policy on AI tools still applies.
